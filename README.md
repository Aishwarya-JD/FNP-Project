**🌸 FNP Sales Analysis Dashboard**

An end-to-end Excel Business Intelligence project that analyzes sales performance, customer behavior, product trends, and occasion-based purchasing patterns for Ferns N Petals (FNP) using interactive dashboards and data visualization techniques.

**📌 Project Overview**

The FNP Sales Analysis Dashboard is a comprehensive Excel-based analytics project designed to uncover key business insights from customer orders and sales transactions.

The project leverages transactional sales data to evaluate **revenue performance across different occasions, product categories, customer locations, and delivery timelines.** 
Through interactive visualizations and KPI-driven reporting, the dashboard helps stakeholders understand **customer preferences, identify high-performing products, and optimize business strategies.**

This project demonstrates practical data analytics skills including:
Data Cleaning
Data Transformation
Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
KPI Development
Dashboard Design
Business Intelligence Reporting
Data Storytelling

**🎯 Business Problem**

FNP operates across multiple gifting occasions throughout the year. Understanding which products, occasions, 
and customer segments contribute the most revenue is essential for improving business performance.

This analysis aims to answer key business questions:
Which occasions generate the highest revenue?
What are the best-selling product categories?
How do customer purchasing patterns vary throughout the year?
Which products contribute the most to total sales?
What is the average delivery timeline for customer orders?
How can the company improve sales and operational efficiency?

**📈 Key Insights:**

**✅ Occasion Performance:**
Anniversary and Raksha Bandhan generated the highest revenue among all occasions.
Festival and gifting seasons significantly boosted sales performance.

**✅ Product Performance:**
Soft Toys, Sweets, and Colors categories contributed the highest revenue.
Several premium-priced products consistently outperformed other offerings.

**✅ Customer Trends:**
Customer demand increased during major festive events.
Repeat gifting occasions showed strong revenue potential.

**✅ Operational Insights:**
Average delivery time remained around 5.5 days.
Delivery performance was generally consistent throughout the year.

**💡 Business Recommendations:**

Revenue Growth
Increase inventory before major gifting seasons.
Launch targeted campaigns for top-performing occasions.
Product Strategy
Promote high-revenue product categories.
Bundle best-selling products into seasonal gift packages.
Customer Engagement
Create occasion-based loyalty programs.
Introduce personalized recommendations using previous purchase behavior.
Operations
Optimize delivery routes during peak demand periods.
Reduce delivery turnaround time to improve customer satisfaction.
